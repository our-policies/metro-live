Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 28, 2026

Welcome to Bangladesh School College List. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a premium experience, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: When you sign up via Firebase Authentication, we collect your email address and profile details to manage your account.
  • Cloud Bookmarks: We store your saved institutions and preferences in our secure cloud vault to ensure your data is synced across devices.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymized data regarding how you interact with the app to improve performance and UI/UX.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide secure access to your account.
  • Synchronize your bookmarks and "Vault" data.
  • Personalize your experience within the "Total Glass" aesthetic interface.
  • Respond to support requests and improve app functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes high-quality third-party services to ensure production-ready performance:

  • Google Firebase: Used for Authentication, Firestore database management, and Cloud Storage.
  • Expo: Used for application framework and universal linking capabilities.

These third parties may collect information used to identify you in accordance with their own privacy policies.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Your bookmarks and authentication data are handled through encrypted channels. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Search & Offline Data

The 54,000+ institutional records (EIIN, names, locations) are stored locally using SQLite for offline-first performance. This data is public institutional information and does not involve tracking your personal location unless you explicitly grant permission for proximity-based searching.

6. Your Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to access or update your account information at any time through the Profile section of the app.

If you wish to permanently delete your account and all associated data (including your synced bookmarks in the "Vault"), please contact us via email at nadesoftwares@gmail.com. Upon receiving your request, we will delete your records from our servers within a reasonable timeframe.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
